WHO WE ARE

About ASW Law Advocates LLP

We are a legal practice based in Nairobi, Kenya, specializing in offering client-centric legal solutions to our clientele, which includes individuals and corporations.

HOW WE WORK

Our Approach

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We listen first, to understand your situation, your goals and what is at stake.

  2. 2

    Advice

    We explain your options and the risks in clear terms, and recommend a practical way forward.

  3. 3

    Representation

    We act on your instructions, in negotiations, before tribunals and in court where needed.
