Frequently Asked Questions

We offer world-class legal representation.

  • How do I book an appointment?

    Use the Book Appointment button on this site, call us on +254 788 685 911 or email info@aswlaw.africa. Our office hours are Monday to Friday, 9.00am to 5.00pm.

  • What should I bring to my first consultation?

    Bring any documents relevant to your matter, such as contracts, correspondence, court papers and your identification. If you are unsure what is relevant, bring what you have and we will guide you.

  • Is my information kept confidential?

    Yes. We treat all client information as confidential, in line with our professional obligations as advocates.

  • How are your fees determined?

    Fees depend on the nature and complexity of the matter. We discuss and agree the basis of our fees with you at the start.

  • Do all disputes have to go to court?

    No. Many disputes can be resolved through negotiation, mediation or arbitration. We advise on the best route for your matter and represent you in whichever forum is needed.

  • Can you help me register a company?

    Yes. We assist with company registration, changes and dissolution, and we draft and review the agreements your business needs.

  • Can you represent me in a tax or procurement dispute?

    Yes. We represent clients before the relevant authorities, the High Court and the Court of Appeal.

  • Do you handle due diligence on property purchases?

    Yes. We conduct due diligence, and we draft, review and negotiate sale, lease and charge agreements.

  • What private client matters do you handle?

    Estate planning, succession, child adoption, child custody, divorce, matrimonial property and mental health matters.

  • Can you advise on employment agreements and disputes?

    Yes. We draft, review and negotiate employment and pension agreements, and represent clients in employment disputes before the Magistrates Court and the Employment and Labour Relations Court.
