Public Procurement and Tax

Public tenders and tax obligations both demand precision. We guide clients through procurement processes and tax matters so that they remain compliant, and we represent them when a decision needs to be challenged.

What we do

  • Offer general advisory services in respect of tax and procurement matters
  • Represent Clients in procurement-related disputes before the Public procurement authorities, the High Court and the Court of Appeal
  • Represent Clients in tax-related disputes before the Tax authorities, the High Court, and Court of Appeal
  • Represent Clients in the High Court and Court of Appeal on Procurement and Tax matters
What we do
  • Offer general advisory services in respect of tax and procurement matters

Property & Real Estate

Property is often a client's most valuable asset. We provide dependable advice on buying, selling, leasing and charging property in Kenya, with careful attention to title and due diligence so that every transaction proceeds with confidence.

What we do

  • Draft, review, and negotiate Leases, Sales, Charge, Discharge and other property-related Agreements
  • Conduct due diligence in respect of property transactions
  • Structure property transactions
  • Represent Clients in property-related disputes at property tribunals
  • Represent Clients in property-related disputes in the mainstream courts i.e. Magistrates, Environment and Land Court and Court of Appeal
